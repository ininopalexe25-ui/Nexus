Chapitre I • Les Origines

A Legacy Forged in the Quiet Sanctuaries of Place Vendôme.

Founded in the winter of 1894 by master horologer Henri de Vendôme, our Maison was born from an unyielding philosophy: that true luxury is not an ornament of vanity, but a quiet manifestation of absolute mastery.

Across four generations, our artisans have remained untouched by the compromises of industrial mass production. In our workshops nestled high in the Swiss Jura mountains and behind the carved stone portals of Place Vendôme, time is our only currency. A single complication watch may demand over six hundred hours of meticulous hand-finishing before receiving its official Poinçon seal.
